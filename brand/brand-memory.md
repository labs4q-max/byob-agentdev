# Brand Memory — Your Business Notes

These are the facts your assistant should use. Copy them into chat with the instructions and emails. A Markdown file is simply a tidy text note.

## Fictional workshop example

```text
Business: Bright Start Studio (fictional workshop example).
We sell ready-made T-shirts for $25 each.
Brand voice: warm, clear, helpful, and brief. No slang or pressure to buy.
We ship within the United States, including Ohio.
Shipping: $5 per order. Standard delivery is 5-7 business days after dispatch; this is an estimate, not a guarantee.
Hours: Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-5 p.m. Eastern.
Return requests: within 14 days of delivery for unused, unworn items. The owner reviews and approves requests.
Custom or bulk orders: the owner must approve pricing, stock, production time, discounts, and delivery commitments. Do not use the ready-made price as a custom quote.
You have no access to orders, tracking, stock, payments, or customer accounts.
If a fact is missing, flag it for the owner. Never invent it.
```

## Make your own copy

Replace the bracketed text with information you have verified. If you do not know something, write "Ask the owner."

```text
Business: [Your business name]
Products or services: [What you sell]
Brand voice: [For example: friendly, concise, professional]
Verified prices: [Your prices, or ask the owner]
Hours and timezone: [Your hours]
Shipping or service area: [Your area]
Delivery or service timing: [Your policy; distinguish estimates from guarantees]
Returns or cancellations: [Your policy]
Owner approval required for: [Refunds, discounts, custom quotes, commitments]
Information the assistant cannot access: [Orders, tracking, stock, payments, etc.]
If information is missing: flag it for the owner; never invent it.
```

Keep the latest version of these notes. Paste it into each new chat, and tell an existing chat explicitly when the notes change. Other chats and tools do not automatically receive updates from this folder.
