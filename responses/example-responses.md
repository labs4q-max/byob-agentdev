## Email 1 — Jordan

**Subject:** Re: Order needed for tomorrow

**Draft reply:**

Hi Jordan, I'm sorry you haven't received a shipping update, especially with your event tomorrow. I don't have tracking details here, so I can't confirm arrival. Your order DEMO-1042 and refund request need an owner review before we can confirm either the delivery status or a refund.

**Missing information / owner approval needed:** Dispatch date, tracking, expected arrival, and the owner's decision on the refund request. Do not assume the order is beyond the delivery estimate: that estimate starts after dispatch, and the dispatch date is unknown.

## Email 3 — Morgan

**Subject:** Re: 30 custom shirts for our launch

**Draft reply:**

Hi Morgan, thanks for considering Bright Start Studio for your launch! Could you share your logo, shirt sizes, preferred colors, and exact launch date? Our owner needs to review the custom order before confirming a quote or delivery timeline, so I can't guarantee arrival yet.

**Missing information / owner approval needed:** Design, size breakdown, colors, exact date, available stock, production capacity, custom pricing, and delivery feasibility. The $25 ready-made price is not an approved custom quote.

## Email 2 — Taylor

**Draft reply:**

Hi Taylor! Yes, we ship to Ohio. Shipping is $5 per order, and our business hours are Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern. Standard delivery is estimated at 5-7 business days after dispatch.

**Missing information / owner approval needed:** None for these questions. The owner still reviews the draft before sending.
