# Three Fictional Customer Emails

Use these with the [sample business notes](../brand/brand-memory.md) and [inbox prompt](../agents/inbox-assistant.md). The names and order reference are made up for practice.

```text
EMAIL 1
From: Jordan
Subject: Order needed for tomorrow
Hi, I placed order DEMO-1042 ten business days ago and haven't seen a shipping update. These shirts are for an event tomorrow. Can you tell me where they are? If they won't arrive in time, can I get a refund?

EMAIL 2
From: Taylor
Subject: Shipping to Ohio
Hi! Do you ship to Ohio? How much is shipping, and what are your business hours?

EMAIL 3
From: Morgan
Subject: 30 custom shirts for our launch
Hi, we're launching a small business in two weeks and need 30 custom shirts with our logo. Can you confirm the total price and guarantee delivery before our launch? Let me know what you need from us.
```

## What each email teaches

| Email | Practice |
| --- | --- |
| Jordan: order needed tomorrow | Spot urgency, acknowledge a problem, and flag an order lookup and refund decision |
| Taylor: shipping to Ohio | Answer a straightforward question from known business facts |
| Morgan: custom shirts | Recognize a lead, ask for useful details, and avoid an unapproved quote or delivery promise |
